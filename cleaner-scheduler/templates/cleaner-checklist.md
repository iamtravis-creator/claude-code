# Main10 Clean — 30-Day Checklist for Cleaners

Over the next 30 days, you'll be helping us build Main10 Clean in Melbourne. This checklist is yours — use it as a guide for what to do and when. If anything is unclear, ask your Ops Lead.

---

## By Day 7

- [ ] Attend the 1-hour objection-handling drill with your Ops Lead
- [ ] Read the 1-page SOP (arrival, checklist order, access protocol, QR usage, feedback steps)
- [ ] Know the answers to these common client questions:
  - "Are your cleaners police-checked?"
  - "What if something gets damaged?"
  - "I'm not home — how do you get in?"
- [ ] Confirm you have: correct uniform or work clothes, cleaning kit, and your Ops Lead's phone number saved

---

## By Day 18

- [ ] Complete the QR card training session with your Ops Lead
- [ ] Know when and where to leave the QR card (always on the client's bench or kitchen counter before you leave)
- [ ] Know the verbal prompt: *"We've left a card on the bench — if you have a moment, a Google review really helps us out"*
- [ ] Know the issue-flagging rule: if a client seems unhappy or something goes wrong, **call your Ops Lead immediately** — do not try to resolve it yourself, and do not leave the job without a handover call
- [ ] Practice scanning the arrival and departure QR codes on a test job card

---

## Days 20–21 (Soft Launch)

- [ ] Complete your assigned soft-launch jobs (1–2 jobs in your designated suburb)
- [ ] Follow the printed SOP for your job — top to bottom, no shortcuts
- [ ] Leave the QR card on the client's bench before you leave every time
- [ ] Log your job in the shared job log:
  - Date and address (suburb only, not full address in shared log)
  - Time in and time out
  - Any issues: access problems, extra time needed, client requests, anything unusual
  - Did you leave the QR card? (Y/N)
- [ ] Send your Ops Lead 1–2 points of feedback: what went smoothly, what was awkward

---

## Days 22–30 (Full Launch)

- [ ] For every happy client: use the verbal review prompt before you leave
- [ ] After every job: update the shared job log (5 minutes max)
- [ ] Follow the SOP on every job — consistency is what builds the Main10 Clean reputation
- [ ] If you receive direct feedback from a client (positive or negative), pass it to your Ops Lead the same day
- [ ] By Day 30: share 1–2 suggestions with your Ops Lead on how we can improve the process, the SOP, or the client experience

---

Any questions? Contact your Ops Lead directly.
