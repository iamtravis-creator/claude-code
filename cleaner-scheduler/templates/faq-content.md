## Category 1: Trust & Safety

**Are your cleaners police-checked?**

Yes — every Main10 Clean cleaner completes a National Police Check before their first job. We also conduct reference checks and a practical skills assessment. You can ask to see a cleaner's credentials at any time.

**Is Main10 Clean insured?**

Yes. We carry public liability insurance covering damage to your property while our team is working. If anything is accidentally damaged during a clean, contact us within 24 hours and we'll handle the claim process with you.

**Will I get the same cleaner each time?**

We always try to send the same cleaner for regular clients. If your regular cleaner is unavailable, we'll let you know in advance and introduce you to whoever's filling in. No surprises.

---

## Category 2: Access

**Do I need to be home during the clean?**

Not at all. Most of our clients aren't home when we clean — they're at work, at school pickup, or out for a walk. We can arrange access via key safe, lockbox, building concierge, or digital lock. Just let us know your preference when you book.

**How do you handle keys and lockboxes?**

If you use a key safe or lockbox, your cleaner will collect the key on arrival and return it before leaving. Key codes are stored securely and are only shared with the cleaner assigned to your job. We never copy keys.

**What if I need to reschedule?**

We ask for at least 24 hours' notice to reschedule a regular clean. For end-of-lease or deep cleans, we ask for 48 hours where possible. Contact us by phone or email and we'll find a time that works.

**What happens if my cleaner is running late?**

Your Ops Lead will contact you if your cleaner is running more than 15 minutes behind schedule. We'll always arrive within your booked window, or we'll let you know.

---

## Category 3: Pets

**Is it safe to have my pet home during the clean?**

Yes — we work around pets all the time. If your pet is anxious around strangers, you might want to keep them in a separate room or outside while we're there. Just mention it in your booking notes.

**What products do you use around animals?**

Our standard products are pet-safe and low-fume. If you'd prefer us to use specific products or avoid certain areas, add it to your booking notes and we'll follow your instructions.

---

## Category 4: Pricing

**How is pricing calculated?**

We price by the hour for most jobs, or offer flat-rate quotes for end-of-lease and deep cleans. The hourly rate depends on the service type: Standard ($35–45/hr) or Deep ($55–75/hr). You'll always get a clear quote before we start.

**What's included in a standard clean?**

A standard clean covers kitchen benches and appliances (outside only), bathroom including toilet and shower, vacuuming and mopping all floors, dusting surfaces, and emptying bins. For a full inclusions list, see the Services page.

**Do you charge for travel?**

No travel surcharge for our standard Melbourne service area (Richmond, Northcote, Fitzroy, Brunswick, Elwood, Camberwell, St Kilda, and surrounding suburbs). If your address is outside our current service area, we'll let you know before you book.

---

## Category 5: Hidden Fees

**Are there any call-out fees?**

No call-out fees. You pay for the time your cleaner is on-site and working — nothing else.

**What about parking or toll costs?**

There's no separate parking or toll charge. Our pricing covers the full cost of getting your cleaner to and from your door.

**What if the job takes longer than quoted?**

For hourly jobs: if a job is running over time, your cleaner will contact you before continuing to ensure you're comfortable with the additional cost. We won't go over time without your approval.

For flat-rate jobs: the quote we give you is the price you pay, regardless of how long it takes.

---

## Category 6: Products & Supplies

**Do you bring your own supplies?**

Yes — our cleaners arrive with all necessary products and equipment: sprays, cloths, mop, vacuum, and everything else. You don't need to provide anything unless you have a specific preference.

**Can I request eco-friendly or specific products?**

Yes. If you'd prefer plant-based or fragrance-free products, or if anyone in your household has allergies or sensitivities, mention it in your booking notes. We'll bring suitable alternatives. There's no extra charge for this.

**Can I provide my own products for the cleaner to use?**

Absolutely — if you have a preferred brand or want us to use your own vacuum, just note it when booking. Your cleaner will follow your instructions.
