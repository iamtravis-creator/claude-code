# Partner Outreach Templates — Main10 Clean

Generate personalised versions with:
```bash
python scheduler.py partner --type real-estate --suburb "Richmond" --contact "James"
python scheduler.py partner --type property-manager --suburb "Northcote" --contact "Priya"
python scheduler.py partner --type airbnb --suburb "St Kilda"
```

Replace all `[PLACEHOLDERS]` before sending.

---

## Partner Type A: Real Estate Agents

**Who to target**: Lettings managers and property management teams at agencies with high rental listing volume in your target suburbs. Look for offices advertising 10+ rental properties on Domain or realestate.com.au.

**Value you offer them**: A reliable cleaner they can recommend to departing tenants (end-of-lease) and new landlords (first-impression cleans before listing). Their clients get priority booking + $20 off first clean.

---

**Value you offer them**: Consistent turnaround cleans across multiple properties. One point of contact, reliable scheduling, and a cleaner who knows their properties over time.

## Partner Welcome Kit (send after a partner agrees)

Once a partner is on board, send them:

1. **Their referral code** (format: `PARTNER-[AGENCY/NAME]` e.g. `PARTNER-ELWOOD-REALTY`)
2. **Offer summary**: "Your clients get $20 off their first Main10 Clean using code [CODE]"
3. **Booking link**: `[WEBSITE]/cleaning/[suburb-slug]`
4. **Your contact number** for urgent/same-day booking requests

Keep the welcome message short — one email, under 100 words.
