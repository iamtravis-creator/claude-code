# Google Ads Copy — Main10 Clean Melbourne

Google Search captures high-intent traffic that Facebook can't reach — people actively typing
"end of lease cleaner melbourne" have decided they need a cleaner. This copy is optimised for
conversion at that moment of intent, not for interruption-style awareness.

---

## Campaign Structure

```
Account
├── Campaign 1: End-of-Lease (Priority — highest CPC, tightest targeting)
│   ├── Ad Group A: Bond-back intent  ("bond cleaning", "end of lease cleaning")
│   ├── Ad Group B: Vacate intent     ("vacate clean", "moving out cleaning")
│   └── Ad Group C: Suburb-specific  ("[suburb] end of lease cleaner")
│
├── Campaign 2: Regular Cleaning
│   ├── Ad Group A: Home cleaning     ("house cleaner melbourne", "cleaning service")
│   └── Ad Group B: Suburb-specific  ("[suburb] cleaner", "cleaner near me")
│
└── Campaign 3: Competitor (Optional — once budget allows)
    └── Ad Group A: Competitor names  (bid on competitor brand terms)
```

**Budget guidance:**
- EOL campaign: $30–50/day (highest ROI — one job = $299–$699)
- Regular cleaning: $15–25/day
- Start with EOL only; add Regular once EOL is profitable

## Ad Copy Rules (Google-specific)

1. **Use the keyword in at least one headline** — Google bolds the matching term, improving CTR
2. **Lead with the differentiator** — "Bond-back guarantee" in position 1 headline, not "Main10 Clean"
3. **Include price in at least one headline/description** — self-selects high-intent, reduces wasted clicks
4. **Match landing page to ad group** — EOL ads → /end-of-lease, suburb ads → /cleaning/[suburb-slug]
5. **Use all available ad extensions** — sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets all improve Quality Score
6. **Pin guarantee headline to position 1** — never let it rotate out of the dominant slot

---

## Quality Score Checklist

Run this before any ad group goes live:

- [ ] Keywords match ad copy (1–3 core terms per ad group, not 50)
- [ ] Landing page headline matches ad headline (same language, same promise)
- [ ] Landing page load time under 3 seconds on mobile (test via PageSpeed Insights)
- [ ] Landing page has a visible CTA above the fold
- [ ] All ad extensions are set up
- [ ] Negative keywords added (see below)
- [ ] Conversion tracking installed (Google Tag Manager recommended)

## Weekly Review Checklist

Run these checks every Monday:

- [ ] Which keywords had the lowest CPC with highest conversion this week?
- [ ] Any search terms to add as negatives?
- [ ] Any ad groups with impression share < 40%? (May need higher bids or better QS)
- [ ] Are any ads below 4/10 ad strength? Rewrite underperforming headlines.
- [ ] Is the EOL campaign getting clicks before 9am and after 6pm? (Reduce bid adjustments for off-hours)
- [ ] Check landing page form submission rate — if CTR is good but conversions are low, the page is the problem

---

## Conversion Tracking Setup

Install Google Tag Manager, then track:

| Event | Trigger | Value |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Quote form submit | Form submission on `/#quote` | $20 (lead) |
| EOL book button click | Click on `.btn-primary` on `/end-of-lease` | $15 (lead) |
| Phone call (call extension) | Call from ad > 30 seconds | $25 (lead) |

Import conversions into Google Ads. Use "Maximise Conversions" bidding only after 30+ conversions/month.
Before that, use "Manual CPC" with bid caps:
- EOL keywords: max $8/click
- Regular cleaning: max $4/click
