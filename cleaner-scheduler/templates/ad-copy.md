# FB/IG Ad Copy — Main10 Clean Melbourne

## Campaign Overview

**Objective**: Leads (or Traffic if lead gen not yet configured)  
**Platform**: Facebook + Instagram  
**Targeting**: Melbourne inner suburbs, 1–3 km radius per suburb, age 25–55, homeowners + renters  
**Budget**: $20–30/day per suburb; start with 2 suburbs from soft-launch data  
**Test budget cap**: $200 for first week  
**Decision rule**: Kill ad sets with CPL > $25 after 3 days; scale ad sets with CPL < $15

---

## Ad Set Structure

One ad set per suburb. Run 2–3 creative variants per set to A/B test.

**Naming convention**: `[Suburb] — [Variant]` e.g. `Richmond — Hook` / `Richmond — Offer` / `Richmond — Proof`

---

## IG Stories Format

Stories need shorter copy and a strong visual. Use a clean image of a tidy home interior.

**Stories primary text** (3–4 lines max):
```
Need a cleaner in [SUBURB]?
Vetted. Insured. Easy to book.
10% off your first clean.
[Swipe up / Link sticker]
```

**Visual direction**: bright, airy photo of a clean kitchen or living room — not a stock photo of a mop and bucket. Use before/after if you have real photos.

---

## Targeting Notes

**Interests to layer** (pick 2–3):
- Home improvement
- Interior design
- House & home (Facebook category)
- Real estate, property management

**Behaviour targeting**:
- "Likely to move" (Facebook/Meta audience)
- "Engaged shoppers" (for offer-led variants)

**Lookalike audience**: Once you have 50 customers, upload your client email list to Meta and create a 1% lookalike for each top suburb.

**Exclusions**: Current page followers (avoid wasting spend on people already in your funnel).

---

## Copy Rules

1. **Never use "best" or "cheapest"** — Meta flags superlatives and they erode trust
2. **Always include the suburb name** — hyper-local copy outperforms generic Melbourne copy
3. **Don't put prices in ad copy** — drive to the landing page where pricing context is clear
4. **Keep primary text under 125 chars** — anything longer gets truncated on mobile
5. **One clear CTA per ad** — don't ask people to both book and refer in the same ad
6. **Use real photos over stock** — if you have before/after or team photos, use them

---

## Weekly Review Checklist

Run these checks every Monday:

- [ ] Which suburb had the lowest CPL this week?
- [ ] Which variant (Hook / Offer / Proof) had the highest CTR?
- [ ] Any ad sets to kill (CPL > $25 for 3+ days)?
- [ ] Any ad sets to scale (CPL < $15, CTR > 2%)?
- [ ] Do any ads need new creative (frequency > 3)?
- [ ] Are landing pages loading fast on mobile?

## Video / Reels Scripts

*Short-form video for Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, and Stories. No voiceover needed — on-screen text works.*

### 15-Second Script — EOL (Text-on-screen)

```
[0–3s]  Moving out of your rental?
[3–6s]  Don't lose your bond.
[6–10s] Main10 Clean — end-of-lease specialists.
        Oven. Bathrooms. Carpets. All included.
[10–13s] 7-day free re-clean guarantee.
[13–15s] Book now → main10.com.au
```

**Visual**: fast-cut before/after clips (2 sec each). Upbeat background track.

---

### 30-Second Script — General Recurring (Voiceover or text)

```
[0–4s]  "What if cleaning just... handled itself?"
[4–9s]  Main10 Clean sends vetted, insured cleaners
        to your home in [SUBURB] — weekly or fortnightly.
[9–15s] Transparent pricing. No lock-in contracts.
        Same cleaner every visit.
[15–22s] "We've been using Main10 for 3 months.
          Never had to think about it once." — [SUBURB] client ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
[22–27s] 10% off your first clean.
[27–30s] Book in 3 minutes → main10.com.au
```

**Visual**: clean home walk-through, cleaner at work (not posed), happy client moment.

---

### Video Production Notes

- Shoot vertically (9:16) for Reels/Stories; horizontal for Feed — don't crop
- Captions are mandatory: 85% of Reels are watched without sound
- First 3 seconds determine if the viewer scrolls — open with the strongest before/after shot or boldest text
- Keep Reels under 30 seconds; 15-second cuts consistently outperform 60-second on CPL
- Raw, unpolished clips (shot on iPhone) outperform high-production stock in this category
