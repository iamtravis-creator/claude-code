# Partner Tracker

> Track every partner outreach contact, response status, referral code, and bookings generated.
> Review weekly. Follow up any partner who hasn't responded after 5 days and hasn't referred after 6 weeks.

---

## How to Generate a Partner Pitch

```bash
python scheduler.py partner --type real-estate --suburb "Richmond" --contact "James"
python scheduler.py partner --type property-manager --suburb "Northcote" --contact "Priya"
python scheduler.py partner --type airbnb --suburb "St Kilda" --contact "Lisa"
```

## Partner Welcome Kit Checklist

- [ ] Partner added to Active Partners table above
- [ ] Added to CRM contact list tagged "partner"
