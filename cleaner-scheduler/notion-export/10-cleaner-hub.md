## 30-Day Checklist (print and hand to each cleaner)

### By Day 7
- [ ] Attend 1-hour objection-handling drill
- [ ] Read the 1-page SOP (arrival, checklist, access, QR, feedback)
- [ ] Save Ops Lead's phone number
- [ ] Know what to say if a client asks: "Are your cleaners police-checked?"
- [ ] Know what to say if a client says: "You're too expensive"
- [ ] Know the access protocol (key safe, lockbox, concierge)

### By Day 18
- [ ] Complete QR card training session
- [ ] Know when to leave the QR card (always — on the client's bench before leaving)
- [ ] Know the verbal review prompt: *"We left a card on the bench — a Google review would really help us out"*
- [ ] Know the issue-flagging rule: unhappy client = call Ops Lead immediately, do not try to fix it yourself

### Days 20–21 (Soft Launch)
- [ ] Complete 1–2 assigned soft-launch jobs
- [ ] Follow the printed SOP for your job — top to bottom
- [ ] Leave QR card on bench before leaving every time
- [ ] Log your job: date, suburb, time in/out, issues, QR card left (Y/N)
- [ ] Send 1–2 feedback points to your Ops Lead same day

### Days 22–30 (Full Launch)
- [ ] Verbal review prompt for every happy client
- [ ] Update job log within 30 minutes of finishing each job
- [ ] Follow the SOP on every job — no shortcuts
- [ ] Pass any client feedback (good or bad) to Ops Lead same day
- [ ] By Day 30: share 1–2 improvement suggestions with your Ops Lead

## Emergency Protocol

If anything goes wrong on a job:

1. **Stop work immediately** if there is damage, a safety issue, or the client is very unhappy
2. **Call Ops Lead** — do not try to resolve it yourself
3. **Do not leave the job site** until you have spoken to your Ops Lead and agreed a handover
4. **Document what happened** — write it down as soon as you can

| Emergency type | Action |
|----------------|--------|
| Damage (broken item, spill on carpet) | Stop. Photo. Call Ops Lead immediately. |
| Access issue (can't get in) | Call client, then call Ops Lead. Wait up to 20 mins. |
| Client unhappy mid-job | Acknowledge calmly. Call Ops Lead. Do not argue. |
| Personal injury | Call 000 if serious. Call Ops Lead. Complete incident report. |
| Suspicious access (unsafe property) | Leave immediately. Call Ops Lead. |

## QR Card Protocol

Every job, every time:

1. **On arrival**: scan the arrival QR code on your printed job card (logs your start time)
2. **After the final walkthrough**: scan the task-completion QR code
3. **Before leaving**: scan the departure QR code (logs your finish time and triggers client email)
4. **Leave the client QR feedback card** on the kitchen bench or dining table

If your phone can't read the QR: take a photo of the job card, call Ops Lead to log your times manually.
