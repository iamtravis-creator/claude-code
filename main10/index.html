REIV-Aligned Room-by-Room Checklist

Every Area. Every Detail. Every Time.

Our end-of-lease clean covers everything on the standard Victorian condition report. Here's exactly what we tackle in each room.

🍳 Kitchen
  • Oven interior — racks, grill, door glass
  • Rangehood — filters degreased, fan blades, exterior
  • Stovetop burners, grates & surrounding surfaces
  • All cupboards & drawers — inside and out
  • Benchtops, splashback, sink & tap fittings
  • Dishwasher interior, seals & exterior
  • Refrigerator space (appliance excluded)
  • Walls — spot clean marks & grease splatter
  • Light fittings, powerpoints & switches
  • Floor — mopped & dried
🚿 Bathrooms & Ensuites
  • Shower recess — tiles, screen, grout, soap scum
  • Bath (if present) — inside, overflow, exterior
  • Toilet — bowl, seat, lid, cistern, base
  • Vanity, basin, taps & mirror
  • Towel rails & toilet roll holder
  • Exhaust fan — cover & blades
  • Cabinet interiors & shelving
  • Tiles & grout — walls & floors
  • Skirting boards & door frames
  • Floor & drain — scrubbed, disinfected
🛏️ Bedrooms
  • Built-in wardrobe — shelves, hanging rail, floor, door tracks
  • Skirting boards — dusted & wiped
  • Windowsills & reveal edges
  • Door frames, handles & tops of doors
  • Light fittings, ceiling fans (if present)
  • Walls — scuff marks & handprints removed
  • Power points & light switches
  • Carpet vacuumed (steam clean extra)
  • Hard floors swept & mopped
  • Air-con filters wiped (wall-mounted units)
🛋️ Living & Dining Areas
  • Skirting boards full perimeter
  • Ceiling cobwebs & cornice dust
  • Light fittings, shades & downlights
  • Walls — marks, scuffs, fingerprints
  • Door frames, handles & glass panels
  • Power points, data points & switches
  • Mantels, shelves & alcoves
  • Carpet vacuumed (steam clean extra)
  • Hard floors swept, mopped & buffed
🪟 Windows, Tracks & Blinds
  • Internal window glass — streak-free both sides
  • Window tracks & runners — vacuum & wipe out
  • Flyscreens — removed, washed, replaced
  • Venetian blinds — slats wiped individually
  • Holland/roller blind surface wipe
  • Curtain tracks & brackets dusted
  • Window reveals & sill edges
  • Sliding door tracks — degreased & cleared
🧺 Laundry & Garage
  • Washing machine recess — walls, hose area, floor
  • Dryer recess & lint filter area
  • Trough & tapware scrubbed
  • Cupboards inside & out
  • Garage floor — swept & blown out
  • Garage walls — spot clean
  • Internal access door & frame
  • Laundry floor — mopped
📋
REIV-aligned standard: Our checklist is based on the Real Estate Institute of Victoria's residential tenancy condition report. We know what agents look for — and we make sure it's done.

Frequently Asked Questions

If your property manager or agent raises any cleaning-related issues at your vacate inspection — or within 7 calendar days of that inspection — we'll return and remedy those specific areas at no additional charge. The only condition is that you notify us within that 7-day window and provide us the agent's written feedback (email, text, or condition report copy).
We accept bookings with as little as 48 hours' notice, subject to availability. We recommend booking as soon as you know your vacate date — slots during month-end periods (especially 28th–1st) fill up fast. Use the quote wizard above to check availability for your preferred date.

Yes — we accept Afterpay, allowing you to split the cost into 4 interest-free fortnightly payments. This is particularly useful when your bond is still held and you need to pay for the clean out of pocket. Select Afterpay when confirming your booking.
