## Section 2: Services Offered

**Primary Service: End-of-Lease / Bond-Back Cleaning**

Comprehensive end-of-lease cleaning aligned with REIV inspection standards, covering:

- Kitchen: oven (interior/racks/door glass), rangehood and filters, stovetop, benchtops, splashback, sink, all cupboards inside and out, dishwasher, microwave
- Bathrooms: tiles and grout, shower screen, toilet, vanity, mirror, exhaust fan, towel rails
- Bedrooms: inside and outside of all wardrobes, wardrobe tracks, skirting boards, ceiling fans
- Living and dining areas: all surfaces, skirting boards, architraves, light fittings, air conditioning filters
- Windows: interior glass, tracks, sills, flyscreens, venetian blinds, roller blinds
- Floors: vacuuming, mopping, spot treatment

**Additional Services:**
- Carpet steam cleaning (hot water extraction)
- Move-in cleaning
- Regular residential cleaning
- Office cleaning

## Section 5: Compliance and Capability Statement

**REIV Alignment:**  
Main10 operates to the Real Estate Institute of Victoria's end-of-lease inspection checklist. Our cleaning process is structured room-by-room to ensure all areas covered in a standard REIV condition report are addressed.

**Quality Assurance:**  
- Timestamped photo report provided after every end-of-lease clean, covering key inspection areas
- 7-day free re-clean guarantee: if your inspection flags any cleaning issue, we return within 24 hours at no charge to the tenant or agency
- Dedicated agency contact for direct escalation

**Communication:**  
- Agency copied on post-clean photo report
- Direct phone line for urgent return visits
- Tenant communication handled by Main10 — agent is kept informed, not chased for logistics
