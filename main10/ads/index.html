  • Keep text to 3–4 lines max — thumb moves fast
  • Put the hook in the first 2 seconds
  • Replace text with real before/after photos once available
  • Add a link sticker (not swipe-up for newer accounts)
  • Run Stories and Feed as separate ad sets — don't cross-place

Video Production Notes

  • Shoot vertically (9:16) for Reels/Stories; 16:9 for Feed — don't auto-crop
  • Captions are mandatory: 85% of Reels are watched without sound
  • First frame matters most — hold something ugly or striking for 0–2s to stop the scroll
  • Raw, phone-shot video often outperforms polished production for local service ads
  • Before/after is the highest-performing creative format for cleaning — prioritise getting real footage
